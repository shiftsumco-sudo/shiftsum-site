ShiftSum

Track your shifts, tips and pay. No subscription. No ads. No account.

ShiftSum is a small, fast log for people paid by the hour or by the shift: restaurant and hotel staff, bar and café workers, retail, healthcare, security, anyone whose pay depends on when they worked.

Get it on Google Play App Store — coming soon

What it does

  • Start / End from the home-screen widget. Tips in one tap. That's the whole routine.
  • Knows your pattern. Forgot yesterday? It asks "Did you work yesterday, 17:00–23:30 like usual?" — you tap Yes.
  • Real pay rules. Overtime, night rate, weekend and public-holiday rates, unpaid breaks, tip-out, your pay period.
  • Check your payslip. Type the hours or amount on your payslip and see the difference instantly.
  • Share a week or a pay period as text (LINE, WhatsApp) or CSV.
  • Private by design. No account, no server. Backups go only where you save them.

Price

Free to use with one job, basic hourly pay, tips, the widget and backup. ShiftSum Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds more jobs, advanced pay rules, tip-out, payslip check, CSV export and app lock.
